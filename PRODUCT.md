## Platform

web

## Users

Recruiters and engineering hiring leads screening candidates for internships and junior software roles. They skim fast, often between other candidates, and judge rigor, credibility, and real shipped work within seconds.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Karim Feki, Computer Engineering student at ESPRIT University (Computer & Mobile Systems), Tunis. It exists to prove production-grade engineering across web, mobile, and backend, and to get the visitor to email him. Success: a hiring lead understands who he is, sees verifiable work, and sends an email.

## Positioning

One engineer who spans native iOS (SwiftUI), native Android (Kotlin/Compose), Flutter, React, and Node/Symfony backends, with shipped internship systems at STAR Assurances and Tunisie Telecom backed by official certificates and public repositories.

## Operating Context

Single-page site at https://karimfeki.is-a.dev. Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, GitHub, CV links, and job applications, on desktop and phones. They verify claims by opening GitHub repos, a YouTube demo, and internship certificates.

1. Proof over adjectives: every claim sits next to a repo, certificate, or demo.
2. Scannable in seconds by a recruiter, rewarding for an engineer who reads deeper.
