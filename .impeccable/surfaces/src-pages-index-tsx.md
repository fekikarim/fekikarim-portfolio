# Surface: Portfolio home (/)

Mode: Experience. Audience: recruiters and engineering hiring leads (internships, junior roles). Action: email Karim. Proof: internship certificates, public repos, demo video. Constraints: keep light/dark toggle, keep certificate preview, keep facts; avoid flashy, generic template, austere.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a signed engineering drawing set. Each section is a sheet with a frame, title block and notes. It refuses the default split hero, glass cards and gradient blobs.

OWN-WORLD: Light is drafting paper (#F6F7F4) with a faint grid, ink #12161C, one drafting blue (#1F4FD1) used for linework accents and the email action. Dark is the blueprint print: navy ground (#0B1E3F) with pale linework. Archivo variable, condensed caps for title blocks and labels, normal width for reading text. Hairline frames, zone markers, leader lines, tables. No cards, no shadows used as decoration.

STORY: A recruiter sees who Karim is and what he builds in one viewport, checks the revision history (experience + certificates) and the project sheets (repos), and emails him from any title block.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A full sheet frame with zone markers. Name set very large at left with role and one-line summary. Right: portrait as FIG. 1 with leader callouts to four disciplines. Title block bottom-right holds status, location and the primary blue "Email Karim" action.

FORM: Engineering Drawing Sheet, position 1 of my ordered list (IMPECCABLE'S PICK, chosen by the user over the rolled #4). seed:b2e7c1ccd3cb690e. Signature: frame linework is plotted when a sheet enters view; content is visible by default. Experience = revision table, Skills = bill of materials, Project features = numbered general notes.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
